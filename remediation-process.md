# Remediation Process

## overview
remediation is the process of closing the gaps identified in the gap analysis.
the goal is to move from non-compliant to compliant before the next assessment,
and to build habits that keep the business compliant going forward.

## phase 1: quick wins (documentation)
the fastest path to compliance is almost always documentation.
most small businesses are already doing the right things — they just
haven't written it down.

tasks completed in this phase:
- drafted acceptable use policy for office systems
- created a written password policy (minimum length, no sharing, rotation schedule)
- documented the incident response procedure (who to call, what to log)
- created an access control log (who has access to what systems and why)

**timeline**: approximately 2 weeks

## phase 2: technical controls
after documentation, technical gaps were addressed with the IT vendor.

tasks completed in this phase:
- confirmed payment terminals were isolated from general office network
- verified antivirus was installed and active on all in-scope systems
- established a monthly patching schedule for all workstations
- removed shared login credentials and created individual user accounts
- implemented a formal offboarding checklist for departing staff

**timeline**: approximately 3 weeks, coordinated with external IT vendor

## phase 3: vendor review
any third party with access to systems that touch cardholder data
must be reviewed for their own compliance status.

tasks completed in this phase:
- compiled a list of all vendors with system access
- requested and collected AOC (Attestation of Compliance) documents
  from payment processor and IT vendor
- documented vendor access scope and review date

**timeline**: approximately 1 week

## phase 4: SAQ completion and submission
the Self-Assessment Questionnaire was completed after all gaps were closed.

- reviewed each SAQ question against documented policies and controls
- confirmed all "yes" answers were backed by documentation
- submitted AOC to acquiring bank

**timeline**: approximately 1 week

## key lessons
- start with documentation — it unlocks most of the other requirements
- small businesses need simple, plain-language policies, not corporate templates
- the IT vendor relationship is critical — they need to understand PCI scope
- ongoing compliance is about maintaining habits, not just passing the assessment
